# Project Constitution: Handball Goalkeeper Stats App

## Article 1 — Product Focus
This product exists to help coaches and team staff capture and review handball goalkeeper performance with a workflow centered on real match observation.

## Article 2 — Single-Device Operation
Version 1 shall support one operator on one device for each active match session. Multi-user collaboration is out of scope until a later phase explicitly adds it.

## Article 3 — Offline-First Requirement
All core workflows in version 1 shall work completely without internet connectivity, including session creation, live event logging, summary review, and reopening past sessions.

## Article 4 — Event Fidelity
Each tracked shot event in version 1 must capture outcome, shot zone, and shot type. Event detail is considered part of the product's core value, not an optional enhancement.

## Article 5 — Detail Over Speed When Forced to Choose
If the product cannot fully optimize both speed and detail at the same time, version 1 shall preserve the completeness and usefulness of recorded goalkeeper data over the fastest possible interaction count.

## Article 6 — Live Tracking Integrity
During an active session, event records shall be appended in sequence and corrections shall be limited to undoing or deleting the most recently logged event.

## Article 7 — Immediate and Historical Value
The app shall provide both immediate post-match review and basic comparison across completed matches, while advanced trend analysis remains out of scope.

## Article 8 — Scope Discipline
Version 1 shall remain goalkeeper-focused. Team-wide outfield player analytics, video workflows, automated event capture, and cloud synchronization shall not be introduced unless the constitution is intentionally revised.

## Article 9 — Traceable Delivery
All implementation work shall trace back to the approved specification, plan, data model, research, and task backlog so that future coding work stays aligned with the intended product behavior.
